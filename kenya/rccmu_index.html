Hard cost is not where the Kenyan CFS argument wins. Cure cycles, formwork rotation, and masonry sequencing dominate the RC+CMU schedule. At Kenya commercial lending rates, those weeks translate directly into developer carry savings, debt service coverage relief, and earlier rent commencement. Validation against operator data shifts the hard-cost ranges; the schedule structure stays.
