Savings Breakdown
Insurance Availability — Not Just Cost
In California, the Gulf Coast, and other high-risk markets, the question is no longer what wood-frame insurance costs — it's whether you can get it. CFS isn't just cheaper to insure — in some markets it's the difference between a financeable project and one that can't close.
Lender & Equity Partner Confidence
Banks and equity partners are underwriting construction risk more aggressively than five years ago. A factory-controlled schedule, documented cost certainty, and a defined delivery window is a more bankable project. Some lenders are beginning to offer better terms on CFS projects specifically.
Asset Value at Disposition
The soft cost savings don't stop at year ten. A quieter, straighter, lower-maintenance building with a better insurance history commands a tighter cap rate at sale. Ten basis points on a $20M project is $200,000 in exit value. The framing decision made in preconstruction shows up in the disposition.
Schedule Certainty — Not Just Schedule Savings
The Trade Savings calculator quantifies what schedule compression saves the GC. It doesn't capture what a one-month delay costs a developer at today's rates — roughly $106,000 in carry on a $15M loan. Prefab CFS makes the schedule predictable, which is what your lender and equity partner actually care about.
Carbon & ESG
CFS is fully recyclable, requires no deforestation, and carries lower embodied carbon per structural unit than CMU. For projects accessing ESG-sensitive capital — pension funds, sovereign wealth, impact investors — this is a real and growing differentiator that wood and masonry cannot match.
Punch List & Certificate of Occupancy
Factory fabrication eliminates field variation. Straight walls, consistent dimensions, and pre-punched holes dramatically reduce the punch list at project completion. Faster punch list clearance means faster CO, faster lease-up, and servicing permanent debt instead of construction debt sooner.
All figures are estimates based on field-validated industry data. Actual results vary by project, site, and market conditions.
