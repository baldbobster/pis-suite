Wood-Frame Comparison Estimate — Important Notice
This is a preliminary scope-to-scope comparison estimate of wood-frame construction costs against the cold-formed steel framing scope captured by the CFS Budget Builder. Wood-frame benchmarks are aggregated from RLB Quarterly Cost Report Q1 2026 (regional indices), RSMeans Building Construction Cost Data, and GHV / Prefab Consulting field-validated wood-frame project history. All figures are estimates only, subject to change based on final design, structural engineering review, market conditions, and confirmed scope. Lifecycle costs (carry, insurance, maintenance, schedule) are not included — see the Soft Cost Savings, Trade Savings, and Pro Forma modules for full lifecycle analysis.
